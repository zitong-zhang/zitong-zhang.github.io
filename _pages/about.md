## 张梓桐

**博士后研究员**

北京大学新结构经济学研究院

### 研究领域

经济增长与发展；宏观经济学；产业链安全；大国博弈；战略型产业

### 关于我

我是北京大学新结构经济学研究院的博士后研究员。2024年于北京大学获得经济学博士学位，2018年获波士顿大学经济学硕士学位，2016年获南开大学经济学学士学位。

我的研究聚焦于经济增长与发展、宏观经济学、产业链安全以及大国博弈等领域，致力于从新结构经济学的视角分析产业政策与发展战略。

## Zitong Zhang

**Postdoctoral Researcher**

Institute of New Structural Economics (INSE), Peking University

### Research Interests

Economic Growth and Development; Macroeconomics; Industrial Chain Security; Great Power Competition; Strategic Industries

### About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute of New Structural Economics (INSE) at Peking University. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Peking University in 2024, my M.A. in Economics from Boston University in 2018, and my B.A. in Economics from Nankai University in 2016.

My research focuses on economic growth and development, macroeconomics, industrial chain security, and great power competition, analyzing industrial policy and development strategies from the perspective of New Structural Economics.
